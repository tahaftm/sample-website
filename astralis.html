◉ Our Story

Born in the Stars, Brewed on Earth

Astralis Coffee Bar was founded on one cosmic truth: great coffee should feel like a journey. We source single-origin beans from Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia — micro-lots that carry the terroir of their birthplace.

Every drink is a precise intersection of science and art. Our baristas are trained in extraction physics, water chemistry, and sensory profiling. But above all, they're storytellers in steam.

We designed our space as a cosmic sanctuary — a place where time slows, the universe expands, and the next idea arrives between sips.
